# S2 · Downtown and junctions

Section: Mon Wharf ramp → Market & Fort Pitt → Market & Third → Market Square → Forbes & Smithfield → Smithfield &
Fort Pitt → Smithfield Street Bridge → Station Square (≈ 2.7–5.3 km). Shared system: **junctions** — real
intersection geometry for the city's street graph, route-agnostic, on for the whole Pittsburgh route.

## What changed

Before, every street was an independent ribbon: at a crossing two ribbons simply lay on top of each other, 2 cm
apart (height slots), with both streets' lane paint and both sidewalks running straight through, and the route's
pale shoulder band crossing every side street. Now:

- **One surface per junction** — a polygon built from the arms' mouths and rounded curb returns (radius 4.5 m on
  residential streets, 6 m on primary/secondary, 9 m on motorway links), plain asphalt, level side to side.
- **Street ribbons trimmed** to the junction edge (each arm at its curb return's tangent point), so no ribbon is drawn
  over another inside a junction. Their lift blends to the junction's over 6 m, so the surfaces meet without a step.
- **Sidewalks wrap the corners** (2.5 m, following each curb return; 2 m on the route's side, like its shoulder).
- **Paint**: a continental crosswalk across every arm with sidewalks (2–6 classes) of a junction of three or more arms,
  a stop line across the incoming lanes (both lanes on a one-way street coming in, none going out). No crosswalk where
  the block is too short for one or it would land in the next junction.
- **The route's own road** in a junction: plain asphalt (no edge or centre lines across the junction), its shoulder
  band cut at the junction's edge, and a crosswalk across the road at each mouth where a street with sidewalks comes in.
- **Merged OSM nodes**: nodes within 7 m (or 0.7 × the streets' widths) merge into one junction (dual carriageways,
  turn lanes), never wider than 34 m; links between merged nodes lie inside the junction and aren't drawn.
- Streets meeting more than 1.2 m apart in height are crossing, not joining. (Decks and ramps now join junctions too:
  see *Road meets* below.)

## How to use it (any city route)

Scene key: `"junctions": true` in the scene JSON (or an object overriding `DEFAULTS` in `js/world/junctions.js`, e.g.
`{ "mergeM": 9, "easeM": 14 }`). Off (no key): everything is exactly as before.

- `js/world/junctions.js` (pure, no three.js — the course QC and the tests use it):
  - `planJunctions(streets, ctx, options)` → `{ list, cuts, spans, options }`. Called once from `cityStreets()` when the
    landscape has `junctions` set (`landscape._junctions`). Each junction: `{ id, x, z, route, s, arms, extra, poly,
    curbs, J(x, z), lift }` — `poly` the surface outline (counter-clockwise in x/z), `curbs` the curb returns (for corner
    furniture), `arms[]` `{ k (street, −1 = the route), u (direction), h (half width), t (trim from its node), a0, dir,
    cls, oneway }`, `J(x, z)` the surface's height line (drawn at `+ lift`). `cuts`: street index → arc-length ranges not
    drawn. `spans`: `{ jn, s0, s1 }` route distances the route's road passes through a junction.
  - `streetPieces(st, cuts)` — a street's drawn pieces; `streetPoint(st, s)`; `streetOverlaps(streets, plan, ctx)` — the
    QC count; `drawnLiftMax(p, plan, maxLift)`.
- `js/world/junction-mesh.js` (three.js): `buildJunctionMeshes(city, plan, { get, roads, walks, liftOf })` (called by
  `CityScene._streetsAndLamps`), `drawnPieces(st, k, plan, lift)`, `junctionPlan(landscape, index, route)`,
  `cutRouteRibbon(mesh, plan, dist)` and `routeJunctionOverlay(route, index, plan)` (called by `world.js`).
- Street points carry `p.jn` (inside junction `jn`: not drawn, height = the junction's) and `p.je` / `p.jd` (in the ease
  zone of junction `je`, `jd` metres past its edge).

### Heights: the hand-off to road alignment (S4)

- **S4 owns street heights and centrelines between junctions; junctions own them inside.** Order inside
  `cityStreets()`: the streets' own profile (ground, decks, the keep-under-the-route clamp — and S4's smoothing, which
  must run *before* the `[junctions]` hook) → `planJunctions` sets every point within an arm's trim distance to the
  junction surface (`p.jn`) and eases the next `max(easeM = 10 m, step / easeGrade = 5 %)` (at most half-way to the next
  junction) back to the street's own profile (`p.je`, `p.jd`).
- A junction the route passes through follows **the route's height** (level across, along its grade), 3 cm under its
  road surface; the route's heights (`route.ele`) are never changed. A street-only junction is a least-squares plane
  through its arms (≤ 10 % slope), pushed under the route's road where it reaches it.
- S4's street step / grade checks should skip `p.jn` points and allow the ease zone up to `easeGrade`.
- **Plan smoothing must keep shared OSM nodes shared**: a node used by two streets has to stay at bit-identical
  coordinates in both (junctions are found by exact coordinates; `st.nodes` / `st.nodeIdx` map them into `st.pts`), and
  should not move more than ~1 m (the curb returns are fitted to the arms' directions at their nodes).

### QC

- Course QC (`npm run qc`) prints, for a city route, `streets: X km of ribbon over other streets, Y km under the
  route's road, Z m into other junctions; N junctions (M on the route)` — with or without junctions, so before/after
  is one scene key apart. New fail check `junction-over-road` (a junction surface reaching the road's surface);
  `street-over-road` now skips points inside junctions (not drawn) and uses the blended lift near a cut.
- `test/junctions.test.mjs`: a synthetic crossing and T onto a route, and the Pittsburgh plan (overlap < 12 km, every
  junction under the road).

## Road meets (wave 2: every place roadways meet, 0–18.3 km)

The user: *"We still have a major issue where roadways meet. Intersections are very disruptive to immersion."* This pass
takes every place roadways meet within ~150 m of the route — street junctions, carriageway merges and splits, ramp
gores, streets meeting decks and the McArdle viaduct, the trail crossing or joining streets — inventories them,
measures them as drawn, and fixes what the junction system owns. Route-agnostic: it all runs inside `cityStreets()`
when a scene has `"junctions"`.

### The inventory and its QC check (`js/world/road-meets.js`, pure)

`roadMeets(landscape, index, route, { reach = 150, from, to })` → `{ sites, counts, issues }`. Each site: `{ kind, x, z,
s, d (from the route's line), streets, jn?, issues: [{ check, value, note }] }`. Kinds: `junction` (planned), `link` (two
street ends meeting end to end), `node` (a shared node no junction covers), `end` (a street end touching another surface,
or none), `route` (a street meeting, crossing or running along the route's road outside any junction), `overlap`
(ribbons drawn over each other at one level, 40 m clusters), `clipped` (a ribbon the clearance tube cuts). Checks:
`step` (a lip: > 9 cm), `gap` (> 45 cm: mouth vs ribbon end, the notch at a link), `overlap` (> 5 m of ribbon over another
by more than 75 cm across), `dangling` (> 1.2 m in the air), `paint` (a street's crosswalk or stop line on the route's
road, the route's crosswalk inside a junction), `clipped` (> 2 m). Everything is measured on the drawn geometry: the
route's curve (`routeLine`), each ribbon's own lift (its height slot), trims, junction vertex heights; corners hidden
under the road or its shoulder don't count. Limits grow with distance from the route (×(1 + d / 25 m): a 9 cm lip at the
roadside, 35 cm at 75 m) — `MEET_LIMITS`.

Course QC: check `road-meet` (`tools/qc_course.mjs`), one finding per issue, **info** level, and a summary line:
`road meets: N sites within 150 m (junction …, link …), M with issues`. It is info, not warn, because what is left needs
source heights fixed (below); `test/road-meets.test.mjs` bounds it (the junction system's own geometry near the route
— junction/link steps, gaps, paint within 25 m — may not grow past 10 sites; all issues under 200).

### How the meets are fixed — the passes, in `cityStreets()` order (`[junctions]` hook)

1. `joinCrossings` — streets crossing at one level with no shared OSM node get one (same coordinates in both), so the
   planner makes the crossing a junction.
2. `densifyAlongside` — points every 2.5 m where carriageways run side by side, so trims follow edges.
3. `clearOverpasses` — a deck crossing over the route lower than the rider's clearance (the builder interpolates decks
   between their ground ends) is lifted to 4.9 m over the road, easing at 6 %, instead of being cut open by the tube.
4. `alignAlongRoute` — streets running along the route (the rest of the street it rides, the other carriageway, the
   other half of its bridge) sit flush, 8 cm under the road surface (under its shoulder), easing back at 6 %.
5. `trimAtRoute` — those alongside streets keep only the strips that show beyond the shoulder (≥ 2.5 m wide; nothing
   else); a street beside the road at another level is trimmed back to the shoulder's edge (two levels side by side,
   never one hovering over the other).
6. `trimOverlaps` — carriageways side by side or merging: a branch (a street ending on another: ramp, slip road)
   yields — its inner edge runs along the parent's edge (a gore that fills itself), wholly inside it isn't drawn — and
   takes the parent's height; peers split the overlap down the middle at their mean height. Ribbons carry `tl` / `tr`
   (metres off their left / right half; `ribbon()` honours them and keeps the texture's scale). Trims are smoothed.
7. `planJunctions` (S2's planner, extended): the route's **drawn curve** (`routeLine`, `denseRoad`) for junction
   centres, heights and route arms; **mouths square to the street where its ribbon really ends** (`arm.mouth`, `cutP`;
   the ribbon's end edge is set to the same direction via `tx`/`tz`); **per-vertex surface heights** (`poly[i].y`,
   `jn.top(x, z)`: each mouth at its ribbon's height, so a ribbon flat across meets a sloping junction without a lip);
   **corner junctions** where two streets meet end to end round a > 50° bend (curb return inside, the outside filled);
   **decks join junctions** (a ramp meeting streets at their level is a junction like any other); **virtual nodes**
   where a street crosses the route at its level with no node close by; ease zones long enough for the grade change
   (no crease on hills); `mitreLinks` mitres two street ends meeting end to end (no notch on the bend's outside).
8. `keepUnderRoad` (with each ribbon's real lift) → `followMovedNodes` (paths, side streets and decks meet moved points
   at their height) → `keepUnderRoad` → `junctionTops` (mouth heights from the final street heights).

Drawing (`js/world/junction-mesh.js`, `city.js`, `city-geometry.js`): junction surfaces and the route's overlay use the
streets' **plain asphalt** (u .05–.22 of the street texture, mapped in world metres like the ribbons) and the street
texture's base colour is now the route road's (`#555c60`), so road, junction and streets are one shade; ribbons, junction
surfaces and the overlay all carry **slope normals** (the road's already did), so they light alike at golden hour; the
overlay follows the road's curve and is drawn with the streets' material (one draw call, as before); paint comes from
the pure `junctionPaint()` (the QC checks the same quads): street crosswalks/stop lines only on real street approaches,
square to their street, never on (or under) the route's road nor in another junction; the route's own crosswalks only at
mouths on stretches where the route is a street (`routeKindAt(s) === 'road'`), never inside a junction. With a plan,
`_levelJunctions` is off (the plan owns heights where streets meet) and slots come from the pure `streetSlots()`.

### For S4 (road alignment) — what would remove most of the remaining issues

- **Deck profiles**: the builder's straight interpolation between ground ends brings flyovers down onto the route
  (Mon Wharf ramps at 2.3–2.9 km, the Fort Pitt / Fort Duquesne ramps at 0.7–1.5 km, West End at 15.7 km). Decks
  crossing over the route need ≥ 4.9 m clearance in the builder (`clearOverpasses` does it at run time now, at 6 %).
- **Decks meeting the ground** (links with steps of 1–2 m at 1.46, 2.81–2.84 km): a deck's ground end should take its
  ground street's height (one height per link node, like junction nodes).
- **Route height at bridge approaches**: at 4.07 km (Smithfield & Fort Pitt) the route rides ~1.5 m above the streets
  it should meet at grade before the Smithfield Street Bridge, so that junction can't join the route.
- **Plan smoothing must keep shared nodes shared** (as before) — `joinCrossings` now adds shared nodes at crossings, so
  anything that moves street points after `cityStreets()` must move both copies.
- Your `street-crease` check skips junction interiors and ease zones (see edits above): please keep that if you
  rework it.

### What's left (open)

- ~92 overlap sites remain (most 25–150 m out, around the motorway interchanges at the Point, Mon Wharf and the West

- Junction clusters whose internal link sticks out of their polygon leave a hole (6.02 km); a few junction mouths

- Load time: the passes add ~1.5–3.5 s on this shared 2-CPU box (≈ 0.5–1 s expected on the M4) to `cityStreets()`;

- Junctions still have no distance LOD; Market Square's plaza crossing is one big junction patch (S3's plaza paving
  would read better).
